01 What is CampusCode?

Where Students Build

CampusCode is an AI-powered hackathon platform — one place to run and join hackathons, instead of juggling many tools. A college hackathon usually runs on scattered tools: forms for registration, WhatsApp for updates, spreadsheets for scores, and manual certificates. CampusCode brings idea screening, team matching, rule bots, and verifiable digital ID cards into one unified arena.
